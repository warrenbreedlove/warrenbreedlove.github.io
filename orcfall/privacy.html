Orcfall Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Orcfall ("the game") is made by Warren Breedlove ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used.

Information the game stores on your device

Your game progress (coins, unlocked difficulty levels, unlocked relics, settings and statistics) is saved only on your device. I do not run any servers, the game has no accounts, and I never receive this information. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads, provided by Google AdMob, when you choose to double a coin reward. To show and measure these ads, Google's software in the game may collect information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • Approximate location based on your IP address
  • Information about your device and the ads you see and interact with
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

Google uses this information as described in its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking: the game asks for permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: if you are in these regions, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.
  • Ads are optional: you never have to watch an ad to play the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

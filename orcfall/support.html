Common questions

How do I play? Tap START, pick a difficulty, then choose three starting heroes. Drag your units to position them on the board and press the play button. Battles are automatic. Win to pick a reward, and clear all 30 floors to win the run.

Where are the tutorial tips? You can show them again from Options > Tutorial Tips > Replay.

The "Double Rewards" ad button says AD LOADING. Ads need an internet connection and are sometimes unavailable. You can always continue without watching one.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game? Yes. Progress is saved only on your device.
